Mate AI: To-Do Planner, Privacy Policy

App: Mate AI: To-Do Planner (Android, package app.taskmate.tasks)
Developer: Task Mate Support Team · Contact: taskmate1support@gmail.com
Effective date: 5 October 2026

In short
  • Your tasks, subtasks, notes and reports stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • If you sign in with Google for Mate AI Pro, our server stores your email, name and subscription status. Nothing else.
  • The AI assistant runs entirely on your phone. Your prompts and replies are never sent to any server.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising data.
  • We do not sell your data.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Mate AI is a local-first app. Everything you create is stored only in the app's private storage on your device: tasks, subtasks, descriptions, due dates, reminders, priorities, categories, reports, AI activity, your display name and app settings. We have no copy of this data and cannot see it.

Your Pro status is cached on the phone in encrypted storage (Android Keystore), so the app keeps working offline. Reports you export as CSV are saved to your phone's Downloads folder and are not shared unless you share them yourself.

2. Google sign-in and Mate AI Pro

Signing in is optional. You only need it to buy or restore Mate AI Pro. When you sign in with Google, the app sends a Google ID token to our subscription server, which stores:

  • Your Google account email address, name and Google account ID
  • Your subscription status: plan, start and expiry dates
  • For Google Play purchases: the product ID, purchase token and order ID, used to verify the purchase with Google
  • Coupon codes you redeem
  • The time you last used the service, and sign-in session tokens

We use this only to verify your purchase and to restore Pro if you reinstall the app or change phones. Your tasks never go to this server. Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or other payment details. All connections to the server use encrypted HTTPS.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Drive sync, the app saves a backup of your tasks to the hidden app data folder in your own Google Drive. It only asks for access to that folder. The backup goes directly between your phone and Google. We never receive it. To delete it, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps → Mate AI, and choose "Delete hidden app data".

4. On-device AI assistant

The AI assistant (for example, "Split with AI") runs fully on your phone, using Gemini Nano through Android AICore where your phone supports it, or a downloaded open model. Prompts and replies are never sent to us or to any cloud AI service. The model is downloaded only if you agree. It comes from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which, like any website, receives your IP address when you download a file. You can delete the model at any time in Settings → AI assistant.

6. Advertising

The free version shows banner and video ads from Google AdMob. Pro removes them, except for the optional "Watch a Video Ad" in Settings. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads. This collection is done by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share data only with the service providers listed above: Google (sign-in, Play billing, Drive, AdMob) and the hosting provider that runs our subscription server. We may also share it if the law requires us to.

8. Retention

Account and subscription records are kept while your account is active, so that your purchase can be restored. When you delete your account, they are removed within 30 days, except for purchase records we must keep for tax or legal reasons. Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

9. Deleting your account and data

  • Server account: email taskmate1support@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Mate AI account". We delete your account and subscription records within 30 days and confirm by email. An active Google Play subscription must also be cancelled in the Play Store, otherwise Google will keep charging you.
  • Data on your phone: clear the app's storage, or uninstall Mate AI.
  • Drive backup: see section 3.

10. Children

Mate AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
